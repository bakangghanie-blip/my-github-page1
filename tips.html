Cybersecurity Tips

Practical advice to keep your digital assets safe

01

Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Never reuse passwords across multiple accounts. Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords (minimum 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols).

02

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Add an extra layer of security. Even if your password is stolen, MFA prevents unauthorized access through a second verification step (SMS, authenticator app, or biometrics).

03

Keep Software Updated

Cybercriminals exploit known vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates for your operating system, browsers, antivirus, and all applications.

04

Beware of Phishing Attacks

Never click suspicious links or download attachments from unknown senders. Verify the sender's email address and look for grammatical errors or urgent requests.

05

Backup Your Data Regularly

Follow the 3-2-1 rule: Keep 3 copies of your data, on 2 different media types, with 1 copy off-site (cloud or external drive). Test your backups periodically.

06

Secure Your Wi-Fi Network

Change default router credentials, use WPA3 encryption, hide your SSID, and create a guest network for visitors.
